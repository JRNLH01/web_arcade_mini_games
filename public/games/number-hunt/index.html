How to play

  1. When it is your turn, choose one number from your own board.
  2. After you call it, tap the fill button or press Space to add X's to your card.
  3. Your opponent must find that number on their own jumbled board and click it to stop you.
  4. Then roles swap. First player to fill their X card wins.
